# the k-shaped economy

This is the **One Minute Blog (OMB)**. An email of good stuff for you to think about over the weekend, that takes a minute to read.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623bf84e83241ec49b548e4/67b8adeb39cab7670f9ac1e8_Screenshot%202025-02-21%20at%208.33.38%E2%80%AFAM.png)

\[you walk in and see me sitting cross-legged, lighting candles, eyes closed\]

_Welcome my friend…I’m Shaan the Seer™ – The Oracle of the East Bay. The Prince of Predictions._

**You:** Oh.. I was actually just looking for a bathroom. But I guess I do have a question for you…

_Speak it._

**You:** Well, I’m pretty worried about AI. It seems great - but I’m worried about the future. Is this going to replace my job?

_Mmmm… I see… a letter._

**You:** A letter?

_Silence! Your words are disturbing my vision… the letter…X?  No… C? no…AH YES. there it is. The letter is clear to my inner eye now. I see the letter K._

**You:** The letter K..?

_Yes. The future is shaped like a K. There are two paths. The first path, like this…_

_\*\*takes out glitter pen_\*\*

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623bf84e83241ec49b548e4/67b8ae0f8ad2299d5da98c7a_7.png)

**You:** Oh sh\*t! 80% of my skills will be replaced by AI?

_Yes dear. You are a horse. And Henry Ford just put a car on the road. The outlook is grim._

**You:** C’mon don’t you think that’s a little dramatic?

_Are you questioning Shaan the Seer?_

**You:** No no, of course not. But wait, you said the future is shaped like a K.. what’s the upside?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623bf84e83241ec49b548e4/67b8ae1c6cb0a8e91b4f4185_8.png)

_You have a choice. Either get replaced by AI, or learn to use it and become 10X more productive._

**You**: Got it. So how _exactly_ do I do this?

_\*\*turns down volume knob of “mystic ASMR summer jams2024”_\*\*

_Listen kid, here’s what you need to do:_

Stop thinking of your job as 1 big thing.

Instead - think of your job as a “bundle of tasks”.

For example, if you’re a sales rep – your job is a bundle of tasks:

- Prospecting potential customers
- Drafting cold emails
- Tracking response rates
- Scheduling calls
- Doing the calls
- Writing notes during the call
- Followup emails
- Drafting proposals
- Updating your CRM
- etc..

How many of those tasks can AI do today? Maybe only 2-3 out of 20 can be significantly boosted with AI today.

But AI is improving fast. Soon, half your tasks will be handled by AI.

Write this down - **in the future being able to “manage robots” will be more valuable than managing humans.**

So learn to use AI tools. Make them your baby-back-b\*tch.

**You:** Make them my baby back wha–?

_You heard me. Now get out of here, I have customers waiting._

**You:** Thanks, Shaan the Seer. I’m going to go do this!

_WAIT!_

**You:** What? Are you having another vision

_Yes… I see … numbers…. 15%....20%...25%...._

**You:** Noooooooooooo!!!

_\*\*takes out iPad\*\*_

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623bf84e83241ec49b548e4/67b8ae2d8117a2584fba08fc_visuals%20(4).png)

_That’s all folks. Back again. Every Friday - the One Minute Blog._

– Uncle Shaan
