_W_ hen I was in college, everyone loved the Duke Chronicle. It was **the** thing. Everyone and their momma read it.

But I never thought about it. I didn't know who wrote it or even consider what was going on behind the scenes. If I was smart, what I would have done is joined those nerds, or make my own.

#### Why? I think this is the perfect **‘starter business’.**

You see, **most college kids are ‘white belts’** when it comes to entrepreneurship. Yet 99% of them try pulling off ‘black belt’ moves (i.e. trying to create the next OpenAI of Google).

What they should do instead, is target **‘white belt’** business. Like a simple newspaper (think of the Onion, but for your local college).

It’s a great exercise in:

- **Learning business operations**– you’ll be running a paper biz
- **Marketing**  – i.e. figure out distribution
- **Copywriting**– i.e. the most important skill to have. Period.
- **Leadership** – i.e. managing a group of other young hustlers
- **Sales** – i.e. monetizing the damn thing

I didn’t do it, but I wanna help YOU (if you’re a college hustler) do it.

**Btw, this is how Barstool started!** Portnoy started a local paper and was physically handing it out to people. Fast forward x years, and it turned into a $500M+ empire.
